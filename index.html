06 · THEMED BRIEF

Migration: open format

Six weeks, one subject. Pitch your idea, define your form. Film, web, installation, performance or print that moves.

Tech fear. No fear.

Some of the best ideas here started with a phone, a record player or a photocopier. Workshops build the rest as you need it. The tools are there to support your ideas, not replace them. Integrating no tech, low tech and high tech, mixed media and combinations of processes allows you to experiment.

  • After Effects
  • Premiere
  • Conductive ink
  • Arduino
  • Processing
  • TouchDesigner
  • Artivive
  • Datamoshing
  • Screen output methods
  • Riso
  • Analogue Techfest
